## Final correction

A 25-pixel native pen move on a compressed mobile Surface map could span more
than the shared local physical-jump limit. Screen continuity admitted it, then
the numerical guard correctly retained an undoable failed prefix. The Torus
adapter now divides that known angular curve into bounded intermediate inputs
before admission. Its physical-speed upper bound is
`hypot((R+r)*deltaU, r*deltaV)`; each segment fits within half the unchanged
local limit. The original endpoints, continuous trace, physical dab spacing,
whole-gesture history and all work/queue caps remain intact. Ordinary desktop
movements need one input, as verified numerically. No shared engine changed.

major radius at most 1,000,000,000 m, and ratio 3-4. Elevations remain bounded
by +/-tube radius/8 and color/shade the reference boundary. Displaced meshing,
excavation, Hollow Torus, Ringworlds and unrestricted shaping remain deferred.

remain separate from this technical delivery. No website integration, hosting,
