The reference solid consists of 64 welded triangular loft spans between rectangular stations. The oriented closed genus-zero boundary has 260 vertices, 774 edges and 516 triangular charts. Two broad regions, two lateral regions and two distinct planar terminal closures share one welded terrain identity. Only real boundary positions alias; a full twist never joins the ends or exchanges the broad faces. Every edge has two opposite incidences. All triangle pairs are checked for unexpected material intersection, including contacts outside the actual common point/edge of adjacent triangles.

Structural bounds: World Size 100,000 to 1,000,000,000 m; length 0.5 to 2 times Size; width 0.12 to 0.35 times Size; station thickness 0.30 to 0.80 times width; 0 to 2 twist turns in either direction; uniform distribution; 0 to 90 degrees planar bend.
