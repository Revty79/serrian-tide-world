## Delivered numerical component

Raise, Lower, Smooth and Flatten now prepare one physical cylinder dab over side, both actual disks, shared rims, angular wraps and cap centers. Conservative local chart windows emit canonical owners once, then a guarded chord only culls candidates. B1 global intrinsic distance enclosures determine influence. A large brush can reach all connected regions and use real cap shortcuts without a three-map authority or an interior 3D shortcut.

Versioned settings use physical radius/strength/target meters and bounded intensity. Cubic influence is enclosed, overlapping boundaries refine, and uncertifiable precision/work/range stops reject the entire dab. Smooth uses positive symmetric finite-volume-style conductances with center fans, unequal polar spacing and sharp-rim dual strips. It preserves constants, has a half-center maximum principle and predictable repeated application. It is a local pseudo-time approximation, not an area-conservative uniform-time heat solver.

Cooperative preparation reads one guarded unchanged source epoch. It retains only changed index/Float32 chunks, with no full authority copy or terrain-sized cache. Explicit commit validates the entire ordered identity-bound patch before writing, once per changed canonical sample. Cancellation, source conflicts, nonfinite inputs/calculations, allocation or numerical/budget failure do not partially apply a dab. Existing saturation, signed-zero and per-word revision semantics are preserved. The [numerical specification](CYLINDRICAL-SCULPTING.md) gives candidate completeness, conductances/areas, interval/error calculations, API/lifecycle and hard limits.
