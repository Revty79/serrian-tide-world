2. **Physical construction.** Let `(u,v,w)` belong to the closed unit ball, `f=1-k*u*u` and `phi=beta*(u+h*(u*u*u-u))`. The physical material is the complete image
   `F=(S/2)*((R+a*f*v)*cos(phi), (R+a*f*v)*sin(phi), b*f*w)`.
   Here `b=a*depthRatio` and `2*beta` is the angular span. Cross-sections have radii `a*f*sqrt(1-u*u)` and `b*f*sqrt(1-u*u)`. The middle is genuinely rounded and the ends close smoothly. The construction does not depend on subtraction or excavation.
