format. Its N256 authority has 262,146 samples; the accepted polyhedral display
cache caps at 196,610 and correctly rejects that size. An operational pass needs
a verified eight-face net, capacity qualification, worker configuration, strict
Save/Open and native input/output-pixel proof. No existing limit was raised.

tetrahedra. Physical holes/tunnels and displaced-terrain meshing remain separate
systems. No artificial connection joins independent inside/outside banks.
