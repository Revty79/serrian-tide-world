# Fragmented Worlds

## Checkpoint A: genuine fragmentation foundation

The reference solid is a regular tetrahedron of axis-aligned width 10,000 to
1,000,000,000 meters. Four cones connect its boundary triangles to one strictly
interior point. Eight deterministic seed patterns permute barycentric weights
22%, 24%, 26%, 28%. Each resulting unequal tetrahedral fragment has one original
outer face and three real exposed break faces. All four surfaces are editable.
This is a partition of one common body, not an arrangement of unrelated solids.

Local coordinates use each fragment's centroid. Initial positions separate
those centroids radially by 15% to 300%. Rotation and bounded uniform arrangement
scale preserve local geometry and elevation words. Convex separating-axis tests
reject intersecting, touching and numerically uncertain configurations. Surface
geography remains in the immutable local reference metric.

Each fragment uses 2*N*N+2 canonical samples: 32,770 at N128 and 131,074 at
N256. Four N256 pieces total 524,296 samples (2,097,184 authority bytes), beneath
the unchanged aggregate component budget. No display or worker capacity cap
was raised. Physical joins have one canonical word; empty space has no aliases.

The existing Floating Islands document, worker/session, brush, perspective
scene, connected editor, atlas, palette/relief and history contracts are shared
incrementally. Floating Islands retain rigid poses and their exact v31/v32
interpretations. Fragment arrangement scale is isolated to the new family.

Validation: twelve targeted numerical/shared tests cover volume partition,
closed convex boundaries, opposite break normals, all bounded map nets,
canonical ownership, intrinsic distances, four brushes, exact history and
isolation. TypeScript and production build pass. Three installed-Chrome
regressions pass: Floating Islands N128 actual WebGL2/Canvas workflows with
exact v32 reopening, and independent physical-world pixel reference checks.
Evidence is frozen in `evidence/fragmented-foundation/receipt.json`.

This checkpoint is a geometry foundation. Fragmented World creation and strict
persistence are not yet exposed as an operational World. No excavation,
custom cutting, displacement meshing or other World family is implemented.
Owner acceptance remains separate from technical validation.

## Checkpoint B: operational Atlas editor

Choose **Fragmented World**. Set parent width, fracture seed, separation and
N128/N256 detail; Atlas creates the four physical pieces together. The World
view uses real perspective, depth and nearest-material picking. Select a piece,
choose **Sculpt selected fragment**, and edit its Physical view or connected
Surface map. Every break face uses the same authoritative terrain as the outer
face. The selected fragment remains visible in the navigation controls.

Raise, Lower, Smooth and Flatten use the accepted worker, scheduling, visible
surface clipping and exact whole-gesture history. Inactive pieces retain their
prepared resources; local strokes do not execute brushes on their neighbors.
Open prepares only the selected display; World view prepares other displays
once when needed. Tool, reference brush settings and fragment histories survive
view/focus changes within the session. Undo stacks are not serialized.

Format **33** stores the parent reference/generation settings, complete surviving
membership, stable component/terrain/surface IDs, strict physical metadata,
poses and scale, selection, controls and exact little-endian Float32 words. The
whole header and payload are SHA-256 checked. All components are validated
before replacement. New-family records cannot overwrite existing saved
geometry or identity; formats 1–32 and reserved 21–24 retain their interpretation.

Installed Chrome used actual AMD Radeon RX 6600 hardware WebGL2 (not software),
plus forced Canvas, at 1440×950. Twelve new-family browser cases passed:
four complete four-tool/isolation/history/v33 workflows, four ordinary painting
cases on all four physical faces, two 20-second held strokes, and two independent
transformed-world ray/pixel comparisons. The reference checks include unequal
scales/orientations, exposed break faces, above/below views and occlusion.
Stable RGB differences were at most 2/255, with zero checked material-coverage,
silhouette or component-picking violations. Canonical words stayed unchanged
through rendering.

## Checkpoint C: complete component authoring and final qualification

The finished creator controls support selection, World Fit/Focus, rotation,
pan/zoom, individual 3D translation, heading/tilt/roll, uniform arrangement scale
from **0.5 to 2**, and removal. Initial generation creates four fragments;
one through four can remain after removal. Removed pieces return through the
bounded arrangement history with the same objects, identities and exact terrain.
Membership/pose history and per-fragment terrain history remain separate.
The initial camera now exposes the physical thickness and real break surfaces.

Arrangement scale is a uniform transform of the reference body and its geography.
Sculpting distances and elevations retain their saved **local reference meters**;
they are not resampled or rewritten. No in-place geometry replacement, detail
conversion, runtime cutting, new arbitrary fragments, general fracture simulator,
joining, displacement meshing, holes, tunnels or links are included. This first
provider supports a fractured **regular tetrahedral parent**, not a fractured
sphere or every possible parent solid. Eight seed patterns are supported.

Final Chrome qualification added 22 successful fragment checks: all six physical
edges and all four shared canonical vertices on both backends; placement,
orientation, scale, removal/replay, exact persistence and continued sculpting;
repeated selection; narrow-gap painting and widely separated Fit/Focus; fitted
and World graphics loss with exact geography through Canvas and hardware
WebGL2 restoration; atomic corrupt-Open rejection and saved-identity guards;
fitted color/coast/silhouette comparisons at both details; byte-exact incremental
versus freshly rebuilt display output; independent transformed-world pixel/pick
checks; trusted touch/pen and two-finger navigation; and a final creator smoke
using nondefault dimensions and the maximum accepted seed.
