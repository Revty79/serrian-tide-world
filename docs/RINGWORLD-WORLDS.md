# Ringworlds

Checkpoint A establishes a rectangular annular solid with one continuous
material boundary: Inner Band, Upper Rim, Outer Band and Lower Rim. Its
versioned `physical-rectangular-ringworld` identity delegates identical physical
charts, intrinsic distance certificates and canonical ownership to the accepted
Open Cylinder implementation. This is one authority, not two hollow banks.

Creator dimensions are the inner Ring Radius, axial Ring Width and radial
Structural Thickness. Supported inner radius is at least 10,000 m; outer radius
is at most one billion m. Width is 15â€“50% and thickness 8â€“25% of outer radius,
each at least 400 m. Unsupported proportions are rejected before allocation.
The bounds deliberately qualify a usable band sampling profile; arbitrary thin
rings are outside this pass. New terrain is the shared -100 m blank ocean.

Detail N128/N256 uses 4N angular samples. Axial cells and half-rim radial cells
are rounded up from outer-circumference spacing, with a minimum of two. Total
canonical samples are `A * (2Z + 4P)`, bounded by 262,144. Inner angular spacing
is 75â€“92% of outer spacing. Physical wall/rim creases share their actual words;
angular endpoint words are never duplicated. Default dimensions (1,000,000 m
inner radius, 400,000 m width, 200,000 m thickness) use 43,008 / 169,984 words.
Existing stroke, history, queue, distance, patch and display budgets remain in
force. No global cap is increased.

The foundation verifies area, volume, normals, metrics, all four physical
creases, periodic ownership, independent band interiors, certified paths,
all four brush arithmetic tools, and exact whole-operation history. The 21
focused numerical checks, TypeScript and production build pass. Raw results
and frozen source hashes are in `evidence/ringworld-foundation/receipt.json`.
No incomplete Ringworld is exposed in the creation UI at checkpoint A.

Operational UI, strict persistence, real browser pixels and measured latency
remain required before declaring delivery. Technical qualification does not
establish owner acceptance or physical-device verification. Excavation, tunnels,
links and displaced terrain remain deferred; the recovery branch is untouched.
